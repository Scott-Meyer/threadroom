How to compose

Drag a piece with your mouse or finger. Dragging does not change its layer; use the front/back controls to change which piece is on top. To reach a hidden piece, choose its named button above. Focus the board or a piece button and use ↑ ↓ ← → to nudge; hold Shift for bigger steps. Home brings it forward, End sends it back, and R resets.
